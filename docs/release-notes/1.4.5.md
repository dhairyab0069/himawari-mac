## New: three more eras, and Customise

- **1990s Portable:** a portable CD player's LCD (the song, the time, a segment progress bar), round rubber buttons and a jog dial; its LCD remote with level bars, a spectrum and MEGA BASS. In the middle, the CD turns under the player's clear lid. Skins: **Silver** and **Translucent Purple**.
- **2000s Pocket Player:** a speaker dock with a pocket player's Now Playing screen and a click wheel; a speaker grille and touch knobs. In the middle, the player itself, with the cover on its screen and a wheel you turn to scrub. Skins: **White**, **Black** and **Colours (Blue)**.
- **Today, Minimal:** glass panes with the song in large, plain type, a thin progress line and a ring to turn; a thin waveform and flat knobs. In the middle, the cover floats on a card. Skins: **Dark**, **Light** and **Frosted**.
- **Style ▸ Customise…:** every style as a live preview, and:
  - **Follow the song's genre** (from Apple's catalog), with your own genre → style rules, or **take the cover's colours** for the displays and lamps.
  - **Animation:** Full, Reduced or Still (the meters still follow the music).
  - **Meters:** the era's own, or needles, LEDs or a spectrum in any era.
  - **Name:** your own name on the faceplates.
  - **Make My Own…** saves a copy of a skin for you to change; **Import…** adds skins someone shared.
- **The disc changer** matches the era: a crate of records, a cassette rack, a CD changer, a grid of cards.

## Install
Open the DMG, drag Himawari to Applications and open it from there (macOS 14.4 or newer, Apple silicon and Intel). The first time, right-click ▸ Open, since it isn't notarized.
