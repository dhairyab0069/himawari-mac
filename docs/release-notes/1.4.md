## New: styles, from the 1970s to the 2000s

Himawari menu ▸ **Style** changes what the side gear and the middle of the music scene look like.

- **1970s Hi-Fi:** a receiver with a backlit tuning dial, whose red pointer travels through the song (click the dial to jump), piano keys, and a big tuning knob that scrubs. The amplifier has VU meters, switches and silver knobs. In the middle, the cover is the label of a record on a turntable, and the tonearm moves in as the song plays. Skins: **Walnut** and **Brushed Silver**.
- **1980s Cassette Deck:** a tape deck whose cassette winds from one reel to the other through the song, with piano keys and a search dial. The graphic equalizer has LED level meters, a segment spectrum and sliders. In the middle, a cassette with the cover on its label, whose reels you can grab and turn to scrub. Skins: **Black Plastic** and **Chrome Metal**.
- **2000s Rack:** the CD deck and stereo analyzer, as before (still the default), plus a **Champagne Gold** skin.
- Every control works the same in every era, and switching styles keeps the song where it is.
- **Make your own skins:** colours, materials, lettering and a few layout numbers, as small JSON files in `~/Library/Application Support/Himawari/Skins/` (Style ▸ Open Skins Folder). The built-in skins are in `Skins/Examples/` to start from. A file with a mistake is skipped, and the Style menu says what's wrong.

## Fixes

- **Short screens:** on screens where the gear is short (a 13" or 14" laptop with the Dock showing), the analyzer's spectrum overlapped the knobs. The meters now give way.
- **Narrow gear:** on narrow side bars, the knobs and the REPEAT / SHUFFLE words no longer overlap (their click areas used to as well).
- **Clicking the desktop** hides the files again after you've taken a screenshot (the screenshot tool left an invisible window over the screen).

## Install
Open the DMG, drag Himawari to Applications and open it from there (macOS 14.4 or newer, Apple silicon and Intel). The first time, right-click ▸ Open, since it isn't notarized.
