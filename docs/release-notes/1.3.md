## New: playlists, and Spotify's own controls

- **The disc changer.** Press the deck's ⏏ button (or ⌃⌥⌘P, or Himawari menu ▸ Disc Changer) to open it.
  - **Apple Music:** all your playlists as discs with their covers. Click one to play it, and right-click to pin it to the top.
  - **What's playing:** the deck's display shows the playlist and the song's place in it ("DHH · 7 / 41"), and the changer lists what's next. Click a song to skip ahead to it.
  - **Spotify:** add playlists by their link (in Spotify, Share ▸ Copy Link to Playlist, then "Add Spotify Playlist from Clipboard"). Their names and covers come from Spotify's public page, no sign-in.
- **Spotify on the gear:** VOLUME, REPEAT and SHUFFLE now work with Spotify through its own scripting. macOS asks once to let Himawari control Spotify. BASS and TREBLE still need Apple Music's equalizer.
- **Better covers from web players:** video titles like "Artist - Song ft. Someone (Official Video)" are read as artist and song, so the catalog search finds the real cover, and the animated one if the album has it.
- **No white haze at the top:** with the Blurred Video fill, the strip above the video, under the menu bar, is now shaded, so a bright video no longer leaves a white blur along the top of the screen.

## Install
Open the DMG, drag Himawari to Applications and open it from there (macOS 14.4 or newer, Apple silicon and Intel). The first time, right-click ▸ Open, since it isn't notarized.
