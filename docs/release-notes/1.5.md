## New: synced lyrics, and your own eras

The last of Beta 1.

- **Lyrics** (Himawari menu ▸ Lyrics: Off, On the Display, or On the Display and Desktop):
  - the line being sung takes the album's place on the gear's display, in each era's own display;
  - **on the desktop**, it shows big under the cover with the next line below. Where the file times each word, a karaoke sweep brightens the words as they're sung;
  - the words come from **LRCLIB** (lrclib.net, free and open, no account), matched by title, artist, album and length, or Apple Music's own lyrics as pages when LRCLIB has none;
  - **Words Earlier / Later** fix the timing of a song, remembered per song;
  - off until you turn them on: turning them on is what sends a song's title to LRCLIB.
- **Your own eras:** a design file lays out the side gear's panels as blocks (a display, buttons, a wheel, meters, knobs, speaker grilles, switches, sliders…) and picks what plays in the middle. Customise ▸ **Make My Own Era…** starts you off. The example that ships is a **1980s Boombox**.
- **Game cartridges:** a design can play a handheld console's cartridge in the middle (`"media": "cartridge"`), a new one for each song with its cover and title on the label, and add a D-pad and face buttons (`gamepad`).
- **Style… opens Customise:** the menu's long list of styles is gone. Pick from the previews, where your own eras get sections of their own.
- **Skins and designs reload as you save them.** Edit a file in any editor and the desktop follows within a couple of seconds, with no Reload. Choosing a style in the menu also updates an open Customise window.

## Install
Open the DMG, drag Himawari to Applications and open it from there (macOS 14.4 or newer, Apple silicon and Intel). The first time, right-click ▸ Open, since it isn't notarized.
